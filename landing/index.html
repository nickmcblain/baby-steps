Features

Everything is included.

Other apps charge extra for these. In Scrunch they are part of the one plan.

Speak it

"She fed for 15 minutes on the left and had a wet nappy." Say it and it's logged. Ask a question instead and it goes to Ask.

Snap it

Photograph the bottle or the scales. Scrunch reads the numbers and fills in the log.

Ask

"Is 4 nappies normal at 6 weeks?" Answers use your baby's real numbers and UK guidance from the NHS and Lullaby Trust. It never gives a diagnosis. If something needs a professional, it says so.

Personal nap prediction

Predictions come from your baby's own wake gaps, not an age table. You get a notification ten minutes before the next nap window.

Dress for the room

Enter the nursery temperature and get the layers and TOG rating that match UK safer-sleep advice.

WHO growth charts

Weight and height plotted against WHO percentiles, a tap away from the home screen. Add multiple children and a partner at no extra cost.
